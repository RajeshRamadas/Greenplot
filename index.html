Service tickets · Residents & vendors

Raise a ticket. Watch it get fixed.

Residents report a problem from their phone. The layout office assigns it to a trusted vendor or staff member, the vendor does the work with proof, a supervisor verifies it — and you are notified the moment it is closed.

Residents: raise & trackPick your plot and the problem, add photos, and get a ticket number like GP-TKT-2026-000127 to follow.
Office: review & assignSet the priority, assign staff or a vendor, and watch every ticket against its response and resolution SLA.
Vendors: accept & deliverNew jobs arrive as notifications. Accept or decline with a reason, then submit before/after photos and the checklist.
Supervisors: verifyApprove the proof of work or send it back for rework. Nothing closes without evidence.
Closure you can seeResidents are told when work starts, finishes and closes — in the app, by push and by WhatsApp.
Feedback & reopenRate the service, or tap “Still an issue” to reopen. Every ticket lands in the property’s history.

Operational workflow

From property request to verified completion.

The same workflow works for inspections, cleaning, repairs, gardening, incidents and vendor jobs.

01 · REQUEST

Request

Resident, owner or admin creates a maintenance, inspection or service request.

02 · ASSIGN

Assign

Staff, supervisor or vendor receives the job with location and instructions.

03 · VISIT

Visit property

Field team arrives, records start time and captures initial evidence.

04 · COMPLETE

Complete work

Checklist is completed and before/after photos or video are attached.

05 · VERIFY

Verify & record

Supervisor approves and the complete digital record becomes searchable history.
