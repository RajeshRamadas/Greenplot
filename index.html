Operational workflow

From property request to verified completion.

The same workflow works for inspections, cleaning, repairs, gardening, incidents and vendor jobs.

01 · REQUEST

Request

Resident, owner or admin creates a maintenance, inspection or service request.

02 · ASSIGN

Assign

Staff, supervisor or vendor receives the job with location and instructions.

03 · VISIT

Visit property

Field team arrives, records start time and captures initial evidence.

04 · COMPLETE

Complete work

Checklist is completed and before/after photos or video are attached.

05 · VERIFY

Verify & record

Supervisor approves and the complete digital record becomes searchable history.
